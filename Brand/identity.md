# C2: Page

**Mark:** A soft, chunky capital P cut from a single sheet of cream paper-glass. Its top-right corner peels forward, showing a coral underside. The counter is a rounded-square viewport that looks through to the sky-colored field behind it.

**Rationale:** A browser shows you pages, and Plain promises nothing extra. So the mark is one page shaped like a P. The peeled corner adds motion and character: you are about to turn to the next thing. It also nods to the Duo, a sheet that folds. It follows the Rex formula: one friendly cream silhouette on a vivid luminous gradient, with the warmth carried by the coral curl. It also keeps the P legible at 60 px. The flap and the body are separate Liquid Glass groups, so the curl casts a real shadow and catches specular light. It avoids Pinterest (script P with a pin tail), Product Hunt (P in an orange disc) and PayPal. It is not a generic document icon either, because the whole silhouette is the letter.

## In the app

- **Accent tint:** Lagoon Blue `#2973FF` for controls and links. Curl Coral is used sparingly, for the one thing that matters on screen: the active tab, the share action and the reload-complete tick.
- **URL bar:** It sits on a Paper-tinted glass capsule. On focus, the capsule gets a mint-to-blue rim (`#33DBC7` to `#2973FF`). The load-progress bar fills in the same gradient and ends in a coral tip.
- **Page-turn motion:** Swiping back or forward lifts the page's corner with a brief coral underside, which echoes the icon.
- **Launch screen:** A Lagoon gradient field with the paper P centered. The corner curls once as the first page loads.
