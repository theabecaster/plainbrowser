# Coin (final)

**Mark:** A thick ring of cream paper-glass around a dawn: a violet-to-blue sky, a coral sun rising over the curved rim of a deep-blue planet, with a thin mint atmosphere line. The ring's top-right edge is folded in along a straight crease, coral side up. A short paper stem drops from the ring's left side, so a P hides in the mark: the ring is its bowl. You see a paper ring with the dawn first, and the P second.

**Rationale:** A browser is a window onto the web, and Plain promises nothing extra. So the mark is a plain paper ring with the web's dawn inside it, and the coral fold adds motion: you are about to turn to the next thing. The P is hidden the way FedEx hides its arrow, so the icon is a symbol first and a letter only to people who look. The fold is a real fold of a flat paper ring: the crease crosses only the band, and the flap is the exact mirror of the paper it removed, so the notch and the flap match. The stem is a band's width wide with a nearly square foot, so it reads as a stem, not a speech-bubble tail. The curl, the ring and the view are separate layers. The curl and the ring are Liquid Glass groups, so the curl casts a shadow on the ring; the curl has no specular highlight, so the flat paper does not look inflated. The view is a flat layer behind the ring. It avoids Safari's compass, Chrome's wheel, Firefox's wrap, Orion's planet, magnifier icons and speech bubbles.

## Color identity: "Paper & Lagoon"

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Lagoon Blue | `#2973FF` |
| Secondary | Shallows Mint | `#33DBC7` |
| Accent | Curl Coral | `#FF6E5E` (icon curl: `#FFB096` at the crease → `#FF6E5E` → `#EC4A57` at the free edge) |
| Surface / mark | Paper | `#FFF6E6` |
| Background (light icon) | Lagoon gradient | `#33DBC7` → `#2973FF`, violet bloom `#6B4BFF` |
| Background (dark icon) | Deep Lagoon | `#0A2133` → `#0F0F33` |
| View sky | Dawn | `#3A2FCF` → `#3F86FF` → `#FFC08E` at the horizon |
| View planet | Night Lagoon | `#1B2A8F` → `#121552`, rim `#9CFFE8` |
| View sun | Sunrise | `#FFF8E8` → `#FF5A4C` |

Dark appearance uses a slightly warmer, dimmer paper (`#FFF8EE` → `#EED8C2`). Tinted appearances drop the background bloom so the field is even, use plain white paper and a grey curl (`#B8B8B8` → `#6E6E6E`) a step darker than the ring, and a dark view with a black planet under a bright sky band and sun, so the ring, stem, fold and horizon all survive the tint.

The icon is generated by `Brand/tools/gen_icon.py` (writes `Brand/AppIcon.icon`); `Brand/tools/render_icon.sh` renders every rendition, the small sizes, the silhouette and home-screen mockups for review. `Brand/preview.png` is the Default rendition at 1024.

## In the app

- **Accent tint:** Lagoon Blue `#2973FF` for controls and links. Curl Coral is used sparingly, for the one thing that matters on screen: the active tab, the share action and the reload-complete tick.
- **URL bar:** It sits on a Paper-tinted glass capsule. On focus, the capsule gets a mint-to-blue rim (`#33DBC7` to `#2973FF`). The load-progress bar fills in the same gradient and ends in a coral tip.
- **Page-turn motion:** Swiping back or forward lifts the page's corner with a brief coral underside, which echoes the icon's fold.
- **Launch splash:** The chosen icon's field with the mark centered, in that icon's colors. Then the camera goes into the P's counter: the window's cut edge folds back all the way round, underside up, and the opening grows until the app fills the screen (docs/decisions.md, "Launch splash: through the P"). The system launch screen under it is plain paper, since it cannot follow the icon.
