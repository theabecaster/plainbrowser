# C2: Page (final)

**Mark:** A soft, chunky capital P cut from a single sheet of cream paper-glass. Its top-right corner peels forward, showing a coral underside. The counter is a rounded-square window that looks out on a dawn horizon: a violet-to-blue sky, a coral sun rising over the curved rim of a deep-blue planet, with a thin mint atmosphere line. The P is one sheet of paper; the window is the web behind it.

**Rationale:** A browser shows you pages, and Plain promises nothing extra. So the mark is one page shaped like a P. The peeled corner adds motion and character: you are about to turn to the next thing. It also nods to the Duo, a sheet that folds. It follows the Rex formula: one friendly cream silhouette on a vivid luminous gradient, with the warmth carried by the coral curl. It also keeps the P legible at 60 px: the curl is a large, saturated coral dog-ear with a cast shadow, and the window is darker than the paper, so the counter stays crisp at home-screen size. The flap, the paper and the window are separate layers. The flap and paper are Liquid Glass groups, so the curl casts a shadow and catches specular light. The window is a flat layer set behind the paper, so the paper's edge and shadow give it real depth. It avoids Pinterest (script P with a pin tail), Product Hunt (P in an orange disc) and PayPal. It is not a generic document icon either, because the whole silhouette is the letter.

## Color identity: "Paper & Lagoon"

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Lagoon Blue | `#2973FF` |
| Secondary | Shallows Mint | `#33DBC7` |
| Accent | Curl Coral | `#FF6E5E` → `#F04E58` (hinge highlight `#FFD9B8`) |
| Surface / mark | Paper | `#FFF6E6` |
| Background (light icon) | Lagoon gradient | `#33DBC7` → `#2973FF`, violet bloom `#6B4BFF` |
| Background (dark icon) | Deep Lagoon | `#0A2133` → `#0F0F33` |
| Window sky | Dawn | `#3A2FCF` → `#3F86FF` → `#FFC08E` at the horizon |
| Window planet | Night Lagoon | `#1B2A8F` → `#121552`, rim `#9CFFE8` |
| Window sun | Sunrise | `#FFF8E8` → `#FF5A4C` |

Dark appearance uses a slightly warmer, dimmer paper (`#FFF8EE` → `#EED8C2`). Tinted appearance uses a grey flap (`#C8C8C8` → `#545454`) and a grey window with a bright horizon line, so the curl and the window both survive the monochrome tint.

## In the app

- **Accent tint:** Lagoon Blue `#2973FF` for controls and links. Curl Coral is used sparingly, for the one thing that matters on screen: the active tab, the share action and the reload-complete tick.
- **URL bar:** It sits on a Paper-tinted glass capsule. On focus, the capsule gets a mint-to-blue rim (`#33DBC7` to `#2973FF`). The load-progress bar fills in the same gradient and ends in a coral tip.
- **Page-turn motion:** Swiping back or forward lifts the page's corner with a brief coral underside, which echoes the icon.
- **Launch screen:** A Lagoon gradient field with the paper P centered. The corner curls once as the first page loads.
