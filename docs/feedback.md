# Owner feedback

Feedback from testing the app, newest first. Each item stays here until it ships; then it moves to "Done" with the build that has it.

## Open

- **Haptics: abundant yet purposeful, every confirmation and denial:** in progress. Plain's haptics are being rebuilt to one language of eight kinds (docs/ui-reference.md, "Haptics"; plan and inventory in docs/research/haptics.md). Phases 0–2 are done: the new `Haptic` (generators on each window, a throttle so one moment never plays twice, `drop` and `failed`, a firmer medium threshold), and the gestures and sheets no other lane touches. The bar swipe gives a firm tap at half-way, a tick if you go back and silence on release; a flick ticks once. The New Tab card and the swipe up into the switcher arm the same way. A gallery card gives a firm tap past its close line and a tick back under it. Reordering ticks per slot and lands with a drop. Zoom ticks per step with a light tap at 100%. A download taps as it starts (a small one that is done at once plays only its success) and plays success or a soft warning with its toast; a failed PDF warns. Find in the side pane ticks when it wraps and bumps with no matches. Settings ticks on a search engine, bumps on a bad custom address, and plays success after Clear History and Data; History's Clear plays success. The intro ticks on an icon tile and plays one success on Let's Go. Phases 3–5 (gallery menus, profiles, the shell's pull to refresh, toasts, error pages, site toggles, the strip and sidebar) follow once the lanes editing those files land. The Duo checklist to feel it by: docs/research/haptics-checklist.md. (Haptics lane; to check on the device.)

- **Sign-in like Safari:** passkeys on sites that support them, and saved passwords from the Passwords app and every password manager on the phone (1Password, Bitwarden and others), as easily as in Safari. Both need Apple's web-browser entitlement; the request is ready for the owner to submit. Until it lands, sites are steered to password sign-in and a failed passkey attempt says why. Done when the entitlement ships and the device checks in `docs/research/sign-in.md` pass. (Sign-in lane.)

The page stays exactly where it was and nothing of it shows behind the bar; the page now meets a soft rounded glass edge instead of a flat paper slab cut straight against it. (Inner top integration lane; to check on the device.)

- **Duo inner: page top goes under the floating controls; keep a slim glass bar with the host when collapsed, like Safari's boundary:** done, in Plain's style. A band of Liquid Glass now runs across the top behind the floating buttons, the address and the tabs, as Safari has there; pages scroll under it blurred instead of sitting bare under the controls. While you read, the band stays as a slim glass strip at the clock's height with just the site ("apple.com") where the address was; the page's top edge is always the band's bottom, so a site's header rests under it and never jumps when the bar collapses or comes back. Tap the slim band (or scroll up) to bring the bar back. The glass is lighter and more Apple-like everywhere (pill and buttons on every screen too), still in Plain's paper, navy and private violet. Safari's frames and the scenarios checked: docs/research/safari-duo-top.md. With two or more tabs the band also holds the tab strip, which starts 10 pt under the clock; private, the band is the same violet glass as the bar. (Inner top lane; to check on the device.)
- **A quick way to open a new tab without opening the switcher, without clutter:** the bar's Share button is now New Tab ("+"), so the bar keeps its five buttons and nothing is added. One tap on any page opens a new tab with the keyboard up; a long press opens a private one (a regular one from a private page). A stray tap costs nothing: close the field without typing and you are back on the page you came from. Share is the last tile in More, where Safari 26 and Chrome keep it too. On the Duo's inner screen the "+" is in the top bar next to Tabs, with one tab or many; the tab strip no longer has its own. Swiping the address bar past the last tab still opens a new tab, now with a firmer tick where it commits. Settings stays in More: it never had a bar button, so moving it would free nothing. Research and the reasons: docs/research/new-tab.md. (New tab lane; to check on the device.)

- **Full-app clarity pass: every state clear to a first-time user:** every page and state was walked on iPhone 17 (light, dark, largest text), the narrowest iPhone and both Duo screens, and judged from a first-time user's eyes (docs/research/clarity-audit.md, 27 findings with captures). Fixed in this pass: the page sheet now shows which mode a page is in (Reader and Translated tiles in the accent while on, and a kept page as a filled star and "Saved" instead of "Unfavorite" and "Remove"); empty Favorites and Reading List show the More button's symbol instead of naming "the page menu"; Favorites says where favorites open; at large text sizes the sheet's captions are whole words again (its grids keep the default size, like a tab bar, and a long press shows a cell large); the summary's Done matches every other sheet's. The rest is routed to the lanes that own the areas (private and profile indicators on a page, the pill's mode word, search sheet overlaps, permission and passkey wording). (Clarity lane; to check on the device.)

- **Private mode: hard to tell when you're in it; confusing to leave:** being private now shows wherever you look. The address bar and buttons turn a deep violet glass in light and dark mode, with a raised-hand mark and the word "Private" before the site (the mark is in the field too while you type, over a dark keyboard that suggests nothing). An empty private tab says "Private" on a dark screen, with "Leave Private" under it. The tab switcher's bar has a labelled "Private | Tabs" switch with each side's count, the side you're on filled (violet for Private): one tap on "Tabs" takes you out. Close All Private Tabs is in the switcher's menu and, with two or more open, in the tabs button's menu. Private tabs are never kept, so the app always reopens out of private. (Private mode lane; to check on the device.)
