# Owner feedback

Feedback from testing the app, newest first. Each item stays here until it ships; then it moves to "Done" with the build that has it.

## Open

- **Haptics: abundant yet purposeful, every confirmation and denial:** in progress. Plain's haptics are being rebuilt to one language of eight kinds (docs/ui-reference.md, "Haptics"; plan and inventory in docs/research/haptics.md). Phases 0–2 are done: the new `Haptic` (generators on each window, a throttle so one moment never plays twice, `drop` and `failed`, a firmer medium threshold), and the gestures and sheets no other lane touches. The bar swipe gives a firm tap at half-way, a tick if you go back and silence on release; a flick ticks once. The New Tab card and the swipe up into the switcher arm the same way. A gallery card gives a firm tap past its close line and a tick back under it. Reordering ticks per slot and lands with a drop. Zoom ticks per step with a light tap at 100%. A download taps as it starts (a small one that is done at once plays only its success) and plays success or a soft warning with its toast; a failed PDF warns. Find in the side pane ticks when it wraps and bumps with no matches. Settings ticks on a search engine, bumps on a bad custom address, and plays success after Clear History and Data; History's Clear plays success. The intro ticks on an icon tile and plays one success on Let's Go. Phases 3–5 (gallery menus, profiles, the shell's pull to refresh, toasts, error pages, site toggles, the strip and sidebar) follow once the lanes editing those files land. The Duo checklist to feel it by: docs/research/haptics-checklist.md. (Haptics lane; to check on the device.)

- **Sign-in like Safari:** passkeys on sites that support them, and saved passwords from the Passwords app and every password manager on the phone (1Password, Bitwarden and others), as easily as in Safari. Both need Apple's web-browser entitlement; the request is ready for the owner to submit. Until it lands, sites are steered to password sign-in and a failed passkey attempt says why. Done when the entitlement ships and the device checks in `docs/research/sign-in.md` pass. (Sign-in lane.)


## Done

- **Support all expected browser gestures like Safari:** the gestures you already had (swipe the address bar between tabs, swipe up for all tabs, edge swipes, pull to refresh, status-bar tap) are joined by the rest of Safari's. A long press on Back or Forward lists the pages it can reach. The tabs button's long press has Close All Tabs (it asks first). Shake, the three-finger swipe or ⌘Z undoes a tab close. On the Duo's inner screen a pinch in on a page opens all tabs, as on an iPad, and the swipe in from the right edge goes forward when there is a page forward, opening the side pane only where there isn't. With a keyboard, Safari's shortcuts work (⌘T, ⌘⇧N, ⌘W, ⌘⇧T, ⌘L, ⌘R, ⌘[ ⌘], ⌘F and ⌘G, ⌘D, ⌃Tab, ⌘1 to ⌘9, ⌘⇧\, zoom, Escape) and Space and the arrows scroll the page. VoiceOver gets the bar's gestures as actions. The pull-to-refresh arrow now runs through Plain's colors (mint, Lagoon, violet, coral; violets in a private tab), faster as it nears the point where it reloads, then fills with Lagoon and spins until the page loads. Each one acts only where its button would. Full list in docs/ui-reference.md, "Gestures and keys". (Gestures lane; to check on the device.)

- **Crash while holding a tab and moving it around (TestFlight 2611280102, Duo inner screen):** fixed. Dropping a tab in the strip made the strip redraw its list inside UIKit's own handling of that drop, which UIKit refuses (it crashes the app on purpose). The strip and the sidebar now tell the tab list where the tab went and redraw a moment later. A dragged tab also lands exactly where you let go of it: the strip, the sidebar and the switcher leave empty tabs out, and a move used to count them, so a tab could land one place off and the strip showed an order the app didn't keep. A tab never moves between your regular and private tabs. Reproduced on the Duo simulator's inner screen (drags across tabs, at the private tabs and quick drags one after another): it crashed before and passes now. (Strip reorder lane.)

- **The page's address lives in the field with Copy, not in a suggestion-like row:** a tap on the pill over a page now opens the field with the page's full address in it, all selected, and the Copy symbol inside the field at its end, as in Arc's address and Safari's field. Start typing (or paste, or clear it) and it is a new search, which opens a new tab as before; tap into the address and change part of it, and Return loads it in the same tab. Copy shows only while the address is untouched and answers with a check mark and a tap of the haptic. The page's row under the field is gone; Open Copied Link, favorites, reading list, recent and closed tabs stay. New tabs and the start screen open empty as before. (Field address lane; to check on the device.)

- **A quick way to open a new tab without opening the switcher, without clutter:** the bar's Share button is now New Tab ("+"), so the bar keeps its five buttons and nothing is added. One tap on any page opens a new tab with the keyboard up; a long press opens a private one (a regular one from a private page). A stray tap costs nothing: close the field without typing and you are back on the page you came from. Share is the last tile in More, where Safari 26 and Chrome keep it too. On the Duo's inner screen the "+" is in the top bar next to Tabs, with one tab or many; the tab strip no longer has its own. Swiping the address bar past the last tab still opens a new tab, now with a firmer tick where it commits. Settings stays in More: it never had a bar button, so moving it would free nothing. Research and the reasons: docs/research/new-tab.md. (New tab lane; to check on the device.)

- **Paper texture barely visible:** the paper now reads as paper at arm's length. It has about five times the fibre (600 faint, gently wandering strands instead of 121) and twice the fine grain, and night paper rises in step while staying weaker than day. Clumps of strands no longer repeat as a faint grid every 85 pt. It still averages to the launch screen's color, secondary text keeps its contrast, and the tile, its memory and how it is drawn are unchanged. Numbers and the strength strip: docs/decisions.md, "Paper texture". (Paper lane; to check on the device.)
