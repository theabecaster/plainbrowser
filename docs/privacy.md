# Privacy

Plain collects no data. It has no account, analytics, tracking, or advertising, and no server of its own.

- **On your phone only:** your history, favorites, open tabs, site settings and website data. Clear History and Data in Settings deletes them.
- **Search:** what you type in the address bar goes to the search engine you choose in Settings, to fetch suggestions and results. That engine's privacy policy applies.
- **Websites:** the sites you visit see your requests, as in any browser. Plain blocks known trackers and ads on every site unless you turn blocking off for that site.
- **Permissions:** camera, microphone and location are asked for only when a site requests them, and your answer is remembered for that site on your phone.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/theabecaster/plainbrowser/issues.
