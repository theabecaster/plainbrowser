# Privacy

Plain collects no data. It has no account, analytics, tracking, or advertising, and no server of its own.

- **On your phone only:** your history, favorites, open tabs, site settings and website data. Clear History and Data in Settings deletes them.
- **Search:** what you type in the address bar goes to the search engine you choose in Settings, to fetch suggestions and results. That engine's privacy policy applies.
- **Websites:** the sites you visit see your requests, as in any browser. Plain blocks known trackers and ads on every site unless you turn blocking off for that site.
- **Browse for Me and Summarize Page:** the summary is written by Apple Intelligence on your phone, never on a server, Apple's included. Browse for Me sends your search to a search engine (Bing if you chose it in Settings, otherwise DuckDuckGo) and loads the top results, as opening them would. It loads them out of sight, with a fresh private store that is thrown away afterwards, so those sites never get your cookies or logins and nothing they set stays on your phone. Their scripts don't run, and known trackers are blocked. Nothing is saved: no history, no tab, no copy of the summary. The same holds in a private tab.
- **Dictation in the search field:** the microphone in the search field turns speech into text on your phone only (Apple's on-device speech recognition). No audio and no text leaves the phone for it; where the phone can't do that, the microphone is not shown. iOS asks for the microphone and for speech recognition the first time you tap it.
- **Permissions:** camera, microphone and location are asked for only when a site requests them, and your answer is remembered for that site on your phone. Site Settings in the page menu shows and changes it. When one is off for Plain in the Settings app, Plain says so the next time a site asks; to know a site asked, it listens for that request in the page only while the feature is off, and it keeps no record of which sites asked.
